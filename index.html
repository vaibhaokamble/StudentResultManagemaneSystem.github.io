About Us

Sant. Rashtrasant Tukdoji Maharaj

Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University (RTMNU) is one of India's oldest public state universities located in Nagpur, Maharashtra. Named after the revered spiritual leader and musician from Vidarbha, it is a proud member of the Association of Indian Universities and the Association of Commonwealth Universities.

About IICC, RTMNU Nagpur

The Inter Institutional Computer Centre (IICC) is the research and development hub of Nagpur University providing cutting-edge computing services. It delivers courses in computer science, software engineering, networking, and database management systems, while maintaining a dedicated server infrastructure for hosting university applications and providing technical support.
